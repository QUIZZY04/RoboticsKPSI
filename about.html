About Our Robotics Journey

The Robotics & AI Excellence Centre at Krishna Public School International was established to bridge the gap between classroom theory and real-world engineering. We empower students to become creators rather than just consumers of technology, fostering a generation that is ready to lead in the age of automation.

Our Mission

To provide a hands-on learning environment where students master the synergy of hardware and software through project-based learning.

Our Vision

To be a regional hub of innovation that produces future engineers capable of solving global challenges using Artificial Intelligence.

Core Values

Persistence, ethical engineering, and collaborative problem-solving are the foundations of every project built in our lab.

The KPSI Learning Philosophy

We believe that "Failure is a Feature." In the world of robotics, the first code rarely works, and the first circuit often fails. Our curriculum encourages students to iterate, debug, and troubleshoot—skills that are essential for any successful career in the 21st century.

Pioneering STEM Education

Our journey began with a simple Arduino kit and a big dream. Today, the Excellence Centre houses advanced 3D printing stations, AI workstations, and professional-grade electronics benches. We focus on AI & Robotics because computational thinking and hardware literacy have become as fundamental as reading and writing. By integrating these tools into the K-12 experience, we ensure our students are not just spectators of the future, but its architects.
