About the Robotics Lab

Our Robotics & AI Excellence Centre provides hands-on learning using Arduino, LEGO, and AI tools to bridge theory and real-world engineering.

Our Tech Stack

Arduino Uno

The heart of our electronic projects, teaching students micro-controller programming and circuit design.

Hardware

Raspberry Pi

Advanced computing for AI and Computer Vision projects, running Python and Linux environments.

Computing

LEGO SPIKE

Engaging younger minds with modular building and logic-based drag-and-drop coding.

Prototyping

Sensors & IoT

Ultrasonic, Infrared, and WiFi modules to connect robots to the cloud and the real world.

Connectivity

Student Project Showcase

Autonomous Rover

A self-driving robot capable of navigating obstacles using ultrasonic sensors and PID control.

Smart Greenhouse

An IoT system that monitors soil moisture and automates watering using Arduino and NodeMCU.

Bionic Arm

A 3D-printed prosthetic arm controlled by muscle sensors (EMG) and servo motors.

Learning Milestones

  • Foundations: Understanding electrons, components, and basic logic gates.
  • Programming: Transitioning from Block-based coding to C++ and Python.
  • Mechanics: Learning about gear ratios, torque, and structural integrity.
  • Innovation: Capstone projects where students solve a local community problem.
