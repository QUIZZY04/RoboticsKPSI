About the Robotics Lab

Our state-of-the-art Robotics & AI Excellence Centre provides students with a hands-on learning environment. We bridge the gap between theoretical physics, mathematics, and real-world engineering through advanced toolsets including LEGO® Mindstorms, Arduino, and 3D Printing stations.

Our Robotics Curriculum

Level Focus Areas Key Learning
Primary (1-5) Mechanics Simple machines, gears, and logic-based block coding.
Middle (6-8) Intermediate Robotics Autonomous robots, sensor integration, and Python basics.
Secondary (9-12) Advanced Engineering Industrial automation, Artificial Intelligence, and IoT.

Why Choose Robotics at KPSI?

Critical Thinking

Encouraging students to troubleshoot and solve complex engineering problems independently.

Global Exposure

Participation in events like the World Robot Olympiad (WRO) and FIRST Lego League (FLL).

Career Readiness

Laying the foundation for future careers in Mechatronics, Software Engineering, and AI.
