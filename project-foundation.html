The Wiggle-Bot

Welcome, Junior Maker! The Wiggle-Bot is a fantastic way to learn about electronics and mechanical motion. This robot doesn't need complex coding; it uses the power of vibrations to dance and move across your desk! It's the perfect first step into the world of engineering.

1. Gather Your Materials

Vibration Motor (3V)
Coin Cell Battery
Scrub Brush
Double-Sided Tape

2. Building Steps

1 Prepare the Base: Stick a piece of tape to the top of your brush or sponge. This will be the "chassis" of your robot.
2 Mount the Motor: Place the vibration motor on the tape. Make sure the spinning weight can move freely!
3 Connect the Power: Use tape to connect the red wire to the "+" side of the battery and the blue/black wire to the "-" side.
4 Let it Dance: Place your robot on a smooth surface and watch it wiggle around!

Science Corner: Why does it wiggle?

This robot uses an unbalanced mass. Inside the tiny motor, there is a small weight that is not centered on the spinning shaft. When it spins fast, it creates a centrifugal force that shakes the whole motor. This vibration is transferred to the brush bristles, making them "hop" and move the robot!

Troubleshooting Tips

  • Not moving? Check if the wires are touching the metal of the battery firmly.
  • Flipping over? Try moving the motor or battery to the center of the brush to balance it better.
  • Wires loose? Use a small piece of electrical tape to hold them onto the battery more securely.

3. What's Next?

Once your Wiggle-Bot is working, try adding googly eyes, pipe-cleaner legs, or even a small paper hat! You can also try using different types of brushes to see which one moves the fastest.
